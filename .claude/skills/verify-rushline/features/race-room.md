# Race room

A race room runs one race for up to eight humans on the `RaceServer` Durable Object in `apps/rooms`. It holds WebSocket seats, steps at 60 Hz and sends every seat each tick. A browser joins with `/?room=<room id>`. `pnpm rooms:race` races scripted seats through the same `openRoomSeat` client the browser uses, each one a real `createRoomClient` over a real WebSocket that predicts, rolls back and compares hashes with the room.

## Sub-features

- `tick-rate` holds 60 Hz: a seat's arrival gaps sit near 16.7 ms and it confirms at least 97% of the expected ticks.
- `agreement` keeps every seat on the room's race: `digestMatched` is true and `desyncs` is 0 with `hashesCompared` above 0.
- `desync-repair` puts a seat whose confirmed race drifted back on the room's race: it asks once, the room sends one race snapshot and writes a `repaired` line, and every hash after it matches, while the rider keeps predicting ahead. The room answers a seat at most once each 300 ticks and three times a race.
- `no-missed-inputs` gets a seat's controls in on time. `missed` in the status counts the ticks the room stepped with no control from that seat, and it is 0 or close to it on a local link. `pnpm rooms:race` fails when a seat misses over 2% of the ticks. `late` counts every copy of a control that arrived after its tick, and a batch resends up to 8 controls, so it overstates what a rider loses.
- `seat-refusals` sends a ninth seat `refused` with `full`, seats a member who arrives after the start in the lobby for the next race, and answers a plain request to a seat with 426 and a malformed room ID with 404.
- `own-room` gives each room ID its own room: another room ID reads as a waiting room with no seats while this race runs.
- `dropped-seat` holds a seat whose socket was lost for the room's seat hold, 30 s at launch, while the pilot rides it and the others ride on, then leaves it with the pilot.
- `vanished-seat` closes a seat the room has heard nothing from for 10 s, as a friend losing Wi-Fi looks, writes its `held` line and holds it like a dropped seat, writes `left` when the hold ends, and the others ride on.
- `lobby-stays-awake` starts a race after the lobby sat longer than the 10 s a Durable Object needs to hibernate. Its fill and deadline timers keep it in memory.
- `away-hold` keeps a member who dropped out of the lobby listed `AWAY` for the room's `awaySeconds`, 120 s at launch, then removes them. It is never shorter than the race's seat hold.
- `leave-vs-quit`: `Leave` in the lobby, or a boot failure, closes with 4410 and drops the member from the lobby at once. A quit from the race, such as `Back to the bar` or `rooms:race --drop-at-seconds`, closes with 4411: the room gives up the bike at once and lists the member `AWAY`. A lost socket holds the bike for the seat hold, also `AWAY`.
- `seat-ownership` keeps one seat per member. A second socket with the same member key takes the seat, and the older tab is refused with `replaced` and reads `You opened this lobby in another tab.`
- `room-config` takes every lobby and race timing from one checked room config: AWAY 10 to 600 s, custom start 10 to 300 s, load deadline 5 to 60 s, public gather 3 to 60 s, public start 10 to 300 s, queue wait 5 to 300 s, race limit 120 to 1,200 s, seat hold 5 to 120 s, and the public race's rules. A config whose AWAY is shorter than its seat hold is refused.
- `fair-start` holds the countdown until every seat has loaded the race, for at most 20 s, so a slow browser does not miss the start. A seat reads `Loading the race` until then.
- `failed-load` takes a rider whose race failed to dress or build out of the race and back to the same room's lobby, with no ready packet, and the other riders' countdown does not wait for them.
- `boot-failure` shows a rider whose game failed to boot `Could not start the game:` and the message, with Reload and Leave, in place of the lobby. That rider holds no seat and sends no ready packet, so the other riders' countdown does not wait for them, and a seat that was open closes with 4410, so the lobby drops them at once instead of listing them `AWAY`.
- `leave-at-door` lets a rider close the page during the door moment. The seat leaves with no ready packet, and the other riders' countdown does not wait for them. The door moment has no Leave button, so closing or navigating the page is the only way out.
- `late-load` starts a slow rider's race after the room's 20 s deadline: the room races on, and that rider reports loaded and joins the race as soon as its own race is built.
- `lobby-names` lists the humans in the lobby by name, with any fill as `and <n> more`, so a rider can see a friend arrive.
- `lobby-round-trip` lists each rider's round trip to the room in ms beside their name, as that rider's browser measured it and sent it in its pings, with a line saying it is the round trip to this room. A far room shows as a high number for everyone in it.
- `race-tape` saves every race's packets to R2, and `pnpm rooms:replay` runs the race again and matches every state hash.
- `race-trace` saves each browser's timing timeline and the room's missed controls next to the tape, and `pnpm rooms:trace` lines them up by second.
- `queue` forms a public lobby of up to eight riders, at once when the eighth arrives or when the oldest ticket has waited 30 s. It runs on one `QueueServer` Durable Object, which refuses an older build. `pnpm rooms:queue --url <rooms url>` fails when a rider who left stays queued, when one rider's two tabs hold two tickets or the older tab is not told, when eight riders do not land in one public room, when a lone rider leaves early or at tier or bike other than the public rule, or when an old build gets in.
- `public-lobby`: a public lobby (a `pub-` room ID) has no host, the server picks its road for each race, and it leaves 45 s after the first rider arrives, or 10 s after if everyone is ready.
- `lobby` keeps its members, host, rules and ready state across races, and starts a race when every member is ready or a minute after the first rider readied, so a friend still on results gets that minute to come back. No player has a start button.
- `lost-room` shows a window that loses the room mid-race `Signal lost` with the hold counting down, and moves back into the same room in place as the same member, with no page load.
- `rejoin` puts a rider who comes back inside the hold on the same bike, caught up to the room's tick through catch-up packets, with `Your bike is up` and their place for 3 s. A rider back after the hold sees `We kept your bike` in the lobby. `Back to the bar` gives the bike up at once. The move carries the rejoin choice `try`, `no` or `taken` in memory on the room view; the address never holds it. `pnpm exec vitest run --project node apps/rooms/test/host-rejoin.test.ts` pins the rules, and the two-window check below shows the screens.
- `friends-link` opens a new room from `Race friends` on the bar's Race tab. `Copy the link` copies a URL that seats a second window in the same room.
- `guest-names` asks for a name once per browser, keeps it in `rushline.guestName.v1`, and shows each human's name on the other humans' result sheets in place of a field name. The server cleans every name, so a tampered URL cannot send markup or more than 12 characters.
- `room-logs` writes a few lines per race instead of one per message, because a log event costs far more than the message it records.
- `join-limits` refuses a client past 60 seats, 6 room opens or 20 queue tickets in a minute with `refused` `limited`, keyed on `CF-Connecting-IP` (an IPv6 client by its /64), and leaves a client with no edge address or a loopback one alone. `pnpm rooms:gate --url <rooms url>` fails when one client gets past a limit or is refused under it.
- `race-id` gives each race a random ID, separate from the room's invite link, shared by every seat. `pnpm rooms:race` fails when the seats disagree.
- `race-report` turns one race's log lines into a readable report with `pnpm rooms:read`.
- `load-test` runs real seats against many rooms, with load recorded and a message cap on the real network, and reports how many rooms one thread holds.
- `back-to-lobby` returns a rider from room results, on the bar's Race panel, to the same lobby and rules with `Next race`. `Leave` goes back to the bar's home panel with no room in the URL. Neither loads the page.
- `race-menu`: Esc in a room race opens the race menu while the race runs on. The bike gets no controls while it is open, `Back to the race` or Esc closes it, and `Leave race` quits the seat with 4411 and lands on the bar's home panel in place, while the other windows see the bike ride on with the pilot.
- `one-page`: every room move, from `Race friends` and the queue through the lobby, the race, results and leaving, happens in one page load. `performance.timeOrigin` stays the same, and the address changes in place to `/?room=<id>&via=<arrival>` (a bare `/?room=<id>` for an invite link) and back to `/`.
- `net-line`: `globalThis.__rushlineRoom.report()` returns round trip, rollbacks, resimulated ticks per second and input to frame, mid-race and on results. Results no longer prints them.
- `seat-phases` moves a seat only through `seatAfter`. `ended`, `left`, `lost` and `refused` are final, so a late message or a later `leave` changes nothing.
- `host-phases` moves the room only through `hostAfter`, and the status route's `hostPhase` reads `loading`, `countdown`, `racing` and `waiting` while its `phase` keeps the two values a tab on the previous deploy can decode.
- `socket-roles` moves a socket only through `roleAfter`. Each socket is `lobby`, `seated` in the race, or `reporting` for up to 5 s after it ends, and the status route's `roles` lists them in the order of `names`.
- `race-end` ends the race and closes every seat with 1000 once every rider is done.
- `time-limit` hands every seat to the pilot at the room config's `raceLimitSeconds`, six minutes at launch, so a rider who stops and leaves the tab open still gets a result and the room ends.
- `browser-race` puts two windowed browsers in one race, each riding its own bike, with the other human on its road.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- From the Race tab, `Find a race` joins the public queue and `Race friends` opens a new room. Both put the name card over the bar first if the browser has no name.
- `/?room=<room id>` opens the invite for that room; `&via=made` skips it.
- A rider who loses the room mid-race moves back into it in place, back on the same bike inside the hold.
- Esc in a room race opens the race menu: `Back to the race`, Sound, Settings and `Leave race`, under `The race keeps going.`

## Driving it with rv

Preconditions:

- `rv doctor --run <run>` passes on each slot that opens a window.
- The Worker runs and stays running. `pnpm rooms:race` prints one JSON report and exits non-zero with its `problems`.

- **Worker.** `RUSHLINE_WEB_PORT=<port> RUSHLINE_ROOMS_PORT=<port> pnpm exec alchemy dev --stage verify` runs the Worker in local workerd with no Cloudflare login. Use free ports from 1420 up, never 1350 or 1351. The rooms port defaults to 1352.
- **Scripted race.** `pnpm rooms:race --url <rooms url>` races scripted seats and fails on any problem it lists.
- **Join limit tool.** `pnpm rooms:gate --url <rooms url>` sends one burst per limit, each from its own `CF-Connecting-IP` in a random `203.0.x` network: seats into one room, fresh rooms and queue tickets, each 3 past its limit, and prints the admitted and refused counts. Point it at the dev proxy port (`RUSHLINE_ROOMS_PORT`), which keeps a `CF-Connecting-IP` it is given. workerd's own port has no edge address, so nothing is limited there.
- **Load and lag tools.** `pnpm rooms:load --url <rooms url> --rooms 2,4,8` finds how many rooms one thread holds. `pnpm rooms:lag --listen <port> --target <port>` puts a slow, lossy link in front of a room.
- **Fights tool.** `pnpm rooms:fights --rooms-port <port> --out <dir>` races two real windows, each through its own `rooms:lag` link, and checks every strike from the other rider's window. To judge a netcode change against its base build with it, follow [netcode-compare.md](./netcode-compare.md).
- **Tick rate, agreement, inputs, refusals, own room, a quit.** Run `pnpm rooms:race --url http://127.0.0.1:<port> --seats 8 --seconds 25 --drop-at-seconds 20`. `--drop-at-seconds` quits the last seat with 4411, so the room gives up its bike at once with no hold; the **Rejoin** recipe proves the hold. `problems` is empty. Seven reports show about 1,500 `ticks`, the eighth stops near 1,200 with `closeCode` 1000, `status.seats` lists seven riders each with `late` 0, and `worstBehindTicks` is 1 or 2.
- **Vanished seat.** Put a TCP proxy in front of the rooms port that keeps the upstream socket open and silent when its client dies, seat one scripted rider through it and one directly, start, then `kill -9` the proxied rider. Within about 11 s `curl <rooms url>/rooms/<id>` reads `waiting` 1 and the log has a `held` line for that rider with `closeCode` 4408. The `left` line follows when the 30 s seat hold ends, about 41 s after the kill. The other rider's `missed` stays 0.
- **Lobby stays awake.** Run **Two browsers** and press `Ready` in the first only. Both lobbies read `Rolling out in <n> s, ready or not.`, and the race starts at the 60 s clock, well past the hibernation window, with both windows still seated. `pnpm rooms:race` readies every seat as soon as the lobby fills, so it starts at once and does not prove this.
- **Live game and rooms agree.** Alchemy rebuilds the web bundle only when its input files change, and an env value is not an input, so `deploy.yml` writes the commit to `apps/web/public/rushline-build.txt` first and fails if the live site does not serve that commit back. After a deploy, open the live game in a room and sit down. A mismatch refuses every seat.
- **A far seat.** Run `node tools/rooms/lag.ts --listen 1443 --target <rooms port> --rtt 1000`, then `pnpm rooms:race --url http://127.0.0.1:1443 --seats 2 --seconds 25`. `problems` stays empty and neither rider rides a tick with no control. Add `--rtt 300 --burst-every 10 --burst-ms 1000` for a bursty link: each burst costs a few misses, because the clock raises its lead when the burst's ping or lost controls arrive and lets the raise fade over 2 s. Add `--upload-ms 150 --upload-after 30` to slow only the uplink 30 s in.
- **Lag tag.** Run `node tools/rooms/lag.ts --listen 1443 --target <rooms port> --rtt 300 --burst-every 12 --burst-ms 3000`, start with `VITE_ROOMS_URL=http://127.0.0.1:1443 rv up --dev --path "/?room=<id>"`, and ride with real keys while steering, because `&autopilot` sends no controls. `[data-slot=dial] [role=status]` reads `Lag` for about 1.5 s after each stall and is gone between them. With `--burst-ms 1000` it stays off. With `--burst-ms 2500` the race stops about 1 s into each stall and `Waiting for the room` shows near the top, below any cop warning, with `Reconnecting in 4 s` counting down, then goes on the first frame the race moves again; with `--burst-ms 1000` it never shows. With `--burst-ms 6000` it reaches `Reconnecting now` and `Signal lost` replaces it.
- **Fights on every link.** Start the Worker, then run `pnpm rooms:fights --rooms-port <port> --out <dir> --links direct,home,mobile,far,bursts,upload --slots 3,4`. Each link is a pair of `rooms:lag` settings, one per window: `home` is 110 ms with 15 ms jitter on both, `mobile` 180 ms with 60 ms jitter and 1% loss on both, `far` puts the second window at 300 ms against 110 ms, `bursts` adds a 1 s stall every 10 s and 1% loss to that 300 ms seat, and `upload` slows only the second window's uplink by 150 ms, 30 s after the link opens. For each link the tool seats both windows in a new room, starts the race, steers the riders alongside each other, and has each rider kick and punch the other. Each window steers from the race it draws, as a player does, not from the room's confirmed race, which runs a lead and a one-way trip behind. A link's `rooms:lag` proxy keeps one queue per direction for all its connections, so a connection opened after another never reaches the room first; a dev build opens the seat socket twice on Join, and without that order a jittery link let the aborted first socket take the seat and leave the window reading "You opened this lobby in another tab." If a rider's bike is wrecked, the remaining bouts stop at once and say so, because that race can't bring them alongside again. It records the victim's window for each strike and then kicks the second rider down. `<dir>/table.md` has one row per strike, and every time in it comes from the two windows sharing the Mac's clock. A strike passes when the room ran it, the victim's screen drew it fully out, and the victim's shove and flinch showed no earlier than that. The shove is the first frame where the victim's guessed race shows the attacker's strike landing, with strike stun or on the tick the room's hit landed, so a bump between the riders never counts as one. A strike that knocks the victim off the bike passes without a flinch. The stage columns split the delay: press to the input packet, that packet to the victim's confirm of the strike, the room's hold (the attacker's own confirm minus the packet and the round trip at the press), the victim's confirm to the strike being drawn, and press to the strike fully out. "Presses the room dropped" counts confirmed ticks that carried the attacker's control without the button it sent, at the tick the client planned the press for, which is the input delay past the tick it was pressed on; until review of PR #198 the probe compared the room's control with the sample made on that tick and dropped the delay the game passed to the client, so the head ran without its send-ahead and a press planned ahead read as dropped. "Attacker drawn off the room's place at the hit" compares the attacker's drawn position, on the frame the victim's guessed race shows the hit, with where the room had the attacker on that tick. The pilot weaves rider 1 0.3 m either side of its lane every 2.4 s, so both windows draw a rider whose steering they must guess. `<network>.json` has `tracks`, one per window for the other rider: on every frame where both the guessed race and the room have that rider riding, `guessed` compares the guessed position with where the room had the rider on that tick, and `drawn` compares the position the cast drew that frame with the room's positions for the same two ticks blended the same way (`errorP95Metres`), and `worstStepMetres` is the biggest jump between two such frames in a row. `guessed` is what a client without settling would draw, so one race compares both. `<network>.json` also lists every fall with its cause, for when the riders never come alongside. The tool does not cover weapon swings, steals or police.
- **Fights on production.** Run `pnpm rooms:fights --server <game url> --out <dir> --slots 3,4`. Both windows open the live game through `rv up --server --user-asked`, with no lag proxies, so the rows read `live` and carry each window's real round trip. Every merge deploys, so check that nobody is racing on production first. Only multiplayer checks use this; vimaan takes no seats.
- **Join limits.** Run `pnpm exec vitest run --project node apps/rooms/test/route.test.ts` and `pnpm exec vitest run --project vite apps/web/test/room-limited.test.tsx`, then start the Worker and run `pnpm rooms:gate --url http://127.0.0.1:<rooms port>`. It reads `seat` 60 admitted and 3 limited, `open` 6 and 3, `queue` 20 and 3, and `problems` is empty. For players during a burst, put a TCP proxy in front of the rooms port that adds `CF-Connecting-IP: 198.51.100.20` to each request head, start two windows with `VITE_ROOMS_URL` on it, and race and rematch with the tool looping beside them: neither window is refused. For the refusal, give a third proxy the address of a client the tool just used up, such as `<network>.10`, where `<network>` is the `network` the tool printed, open a window on it at `/?room=<new id>`, and it reads `Too many riders joined from your network. Try again in a minute.` with Back and no Reload.
- **Old build refused.** `pnpm rooms:race` opens one seat that claims another build. `problems` stays empty only if that seat reads `build`, and the room's log has a `refused` line with reason `build`. In a browser the room screen reads `The game has updated. Reload to join.` with a Reload item.
- **Lobby.** `pnpm exec vitest run --project node apps/rooms/test/lobby.test.ts` covers the rules. In two windows on one room: the first arrives through `Race friends` and is `HOST`, and `You set the rules` opens the rule choices. The second opens the link, reads the rules and presses `Join`, and `The rules` shows them in the bar. Pick a road and traffic in the first and read them in the second. Press `Ready` in one, `Rolling out in <n> s, ready or not.` appears, and a rule change clears it. Press `Ready` in both and the race starts at once on the chosen road.
- **Room restart.** Start a two-window race and ride 10 s, then restart the Worker with `touch apps/rooms/src/worker.ts` under `alchemy dev`. Both windows read `Signal lost` for a moment, move back into the room with the rejoin choice `try`, show `Getting you back on` and then `Your bike is up`, and the race goes on from the last checkpoint, at most 5 s back, with both riders on it. The Rooms log has one `held` per rider with no close code at the checkpoint tick, then a `restored` line with that tick and `afterMs`, then a `rejoined` per rider. Both windows end on results with the same finishing order. Restart the Worker again while the lobby waits with the windows in it: both show `Reconnecting to the room` and come back into the same lobby in place, with no reload: the same names in the same colours, the host unchanged and the rule kept. Restart during loading instead: both windows land in that lobby under `Room restarted. That race is gone`, and the log has a `lost` line with tick 0. With scripted seats, `pnpm rooms:race --seats 2 --seconds 60` and a `touch` at 20 s shows the seats closed with 1006 and a `restored` line, because the tool does not rejoin. A window whose socket hangs with no close moves back the same way after 5 s of silence.
- **Race end.** Run `pnpm rooms:race --url http://127.0.0.1:<port> --seats 2 --seconds 400`. It takes a few minutes. Both reports show `ended` true and `closeCode` 1000, and `status.phase` is `waiting` with the lobby still holding its rule.

A window on a 350 ms link goes green about 170 ms before a window on a local link, because its controls for tick 1 must leave earlier to reach the room in time.

Within a second the first window reads the same line and sends no binary frame whose first byte is 6, and the Rooms log has a `left` line for that rider at `tick` 0 with `closeCode` 4410, then a `countdown` a few milliseconds later with an empty `stillLoading`; its `waitedMs` is the time from the start to the failure, or about 5000 if the failure came first. ## Gotchas

- `rv throttle --network` does not slow the race. Chrome's network emulation leaves the game's WebSocket alone, and `__rushlineRoom.rttMs()` stays at 1 to 2 ms under `slow4g` and `3g`. Use `rooms:lag` for any multiplayer network test, and read `rttMs()` to confirm the link is real.
- These recipes do not prove a deployed room. Cloudflare's scheduling and `locationHint` placement are untested here. Point `--url` at the deployed Worker to test them.
- Not proven here: real network lag, jitter or loss. A local link hides lag, and a clock that led by half the round trip passed every local run. A throwaway TCP proxy adding 100 and 200 ms round trips gave `late` 0 and 0 desyncs, and that clock lost every control at 100 ms. `pnpm rooms:lag` adds lag for that.
- Not proven here: how a rollback looks over a real network. On a local link the other rider's largest frame-to-frame sideways move was 7.1 cm over 88 rollbacks.
- Not proven here: input to screen. `Input to frame` runs from the key event to the start of the frame after the one that drew the change. It does not include the compositor or the display, and a gamepad has no key event, so a gamepad rider reads 0 samples.
