# Road hazards

Oil slicks, small rocks, and potholes lie on the road, mostly where a rider is setting up or leaving a bend or just past a crest. Nothing warns of them: no sign, no marker. A slick is a dark flat patch with a faint coloured sheen, a rock a grey stone, a pothole a dark hole. Riding straight over an oil slick is safe; a rock bumps the bike into the air and costs speed, and throws the rider only at about 210 km/h or when they are already steering hard; a pothole costs a few per cent of speed and throws only a bike leaned hard over above 175 km/h. A rider who misses a hazard loses nothing. Only riders meet hazards; traffic drives over them.

## Sub-features

- `hazard-placement` puts each hazard where the track schedules it, by road.
- `hazard-look` draws the slick, the rock, and the pothole on the road.
- `hazard-oil` is safe straight across and slips a bike that holds the bars hard over.
- `hazard-rock` staggers a slow bike and bumps a fast one into the air, and throws the rider at about 210 km/h.
- `hazard-pothole` jolts the bike and costs a few per cent of its speed.
- `hazard-miss` leaves a rider who passes beside a hazard with no consequence.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Ride any road. At tier 1 Breakwater Run has 15 hazards, Slag Row 6 and Ridge Road 5, and every hazard is in the same place with the same id in every race.
- Breakwater Run's first hazard is a pothole at 661 m, its first rock is at 2,560 m, and its first oil slick is at 3,420 m, in the outer lane on a bend exit.
- Cut a bend close and the apex pothole is there; run wide on the exit and an oil slick or a rock is there.

## Driving it with rv

Preconditions:

- `rv doctor --run <run>` passes on a development build (every flag below is DEV only).
- Check the schedule first, from the checkout under test, with no browser: `node --input-type=module -e 'import { roadHalfWidthAt } from "./packages/track/src/districts.ts"; import { roads, trackFor } from "./packages/track/src/roads.ts"; for (const id of Object.keys(roads)) { const track = trackFor(id); console.log(id, track.length, "bad:", track.hazards.filter((hazard) => hazard.rarity < 0.6).filter((hazard) => hazard.s < 450 || hazard.s > track.length - 150 || Math.abs(hazard.x) > roadHalfWidthAt(track, hazard.s)).length); for (const hazard of track.hazards.filter((hazard) => hazard.rarity < 0.6)) { console.log("  " + hazard.id + " " + hazard.kind + " " + hazard.setting + " s=" + Math.round(hazard.s) + " x=" + hazard.x.toFixed(2)); } }'`.
- **Flags.** The hazard flags used below, all DEV: `hazard=<id>` puts the player on that hazard's line, `before=<m>` metres before it (default 150); `kmh=<n>` sets the starting speed; `straight` holds that line at full throttle; `clear` moves traffic out of the way; `hold=near&holdafter=<m>` freezes the race when the hazard is that many metres ahead; `hold=hazard` freezes it on the player's first contact with any hazard; `hold=nearMiss` freezes it when the player passes a hazard within 0.8 m without touching it; `hold=crash` freezes on a crash. `holdafter` on the event holds is a number of ticks (60 a second) after the event. All of them need `&prof` for `rv sample` and the tick. Each freeze sets `__rushlineHeld`; `rv wait --run <run> held` waits for it.
- **Reading a contact.** Read the speed with `document.querySelector("[role=meter][aria-label=Speed]").getAttribute("aria-valuenow")`. The page exposes no race state: it has no hazard list, no event log, and no lean. A frozen frame still shows the speed from before the contact, so read what a contact did by releasing and sampling the meter. Call this `SERIES` below: `globalThis.__rushlineRelease(), new Promise((done) => { const speeds = []; const id = setInterval(() => { speeds.push(Number(document.querySelector("[role=meter][aria-label=Speed]").getAttribute("aria-valuenow"))); if (speeds.length === 40) { clearInterval(id); done({ speeds: speeds.join(" "), worstStep: Math.min(...speeds.slice(1).map((speed, at) => speed - speeds[at])) }); } }, 50); })`. Pass it to `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. Speed climbs a few km/h a second under throttle, so a contact is a single step of -4 km/h or lower in `worstStep`; the proof runs without contact read 0, and steering drag alone cost at most 2 km/h in a step. `SERIES` also catches the step a crash makes (167 to 92 km/h in one step on oil, below). The meter alone cannot tell a bump from a crash; `document.body.innerText.includes("WIPEOUT")` can, while the crash caption is up.
- **Where they lie.** Run the node command above. It lists tier 1's hazards, the ones whose `rarity` falls under that tier's 60% share. Tier 2 places those under 80% and tier 3 places every candidate. For Breakwater Run it prints 15 hazards, from `hazard-1` pothole at 661 m x 5.94 to `hazard-23` oil at 7,700 m x 1.47, with `bad: 0`; the recipes below use `hazard-7`, a rock at 2,560 m x 0.98, and `hazard-10`, oil at 3,420 m x 9.29. Compare every road with the table under "Where hazards lie" in `docs/design/tracks.md`; each hazard's kind, setting, and position must match and `bad` must be 0. Change `0.6` to `0.8` or `1` to see tier 2's or tier 3's hazards, with tier 1's unmoved.
- **The game has the same hazard.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-10&before=60&kmh=100&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> placement-oil`. It freezes about four seconds after the page opens. An id the race does not have never freezes, because the player is then left at the start line: a freeze proves the race holds `hazard-10` and that it lies 22 m ahead of a bike placed 60 m before 3,420 m and riding in its lane.
- **It draws: oil.** The frame from the step above shows a small dark flat slick on the road about 22 m ahead in the right-hand lane, on the bike's line, with the bike near the right edge line.
- **It draws: rock.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-7&before=60&kmh=60&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> draws-rock`. A small grey lump sits in the lane next to the centre line, ahead of the bike. It is a few pixels wide at 22 m by design (hazards are not made to stand out), so look for it in the full-size PNG; the near-miss frame below shows the rock at full size.
- **It draws: pothole.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-3&before=60&kmh=60&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> draws-pothole`. A dark thin hole lies on the road ahead, close to the centre line, at night; it is the hardest to see of the three.
- **Oil, straight over it.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-10&before=60&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=hazard&holdafter=10" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20` (the freeze proves the bike touched a hazard), `rv shot --run <run> oil-contact`, then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The slick lies behind the bike in the frame, the speed series keeps climbing with no step down (worstStep 0), and no WIPEOUT appears.
- **Oil, bars hard over: a wipeout.** The crash branch is in `packages/sim/src/rider-drive.ts`: steer of 0.8 or more for 18 ticks (0.3 s) while slip is above 0.5 and speed above 26 m/s. `swerve` forces the steer, so it reaches that branch: the slick is at 3,420 m, so start the swerve 7 m before it and hold it for 60 m. Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-10&before=60&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&swerve=3413&swervefor=60&swerveto=-1&hold=hazard&holdafter=10" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> oil-hard-contact`, then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed series falls by tens of km/h in one step and keeps falling, the slide of a downed rider. Then run the same URL with `hold=crash&holdafter=20`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> oil-crash`: it freezes about four seconds in with WIPEOUT over the road (`document.body.innerText` ended `WIPEOUT`). The control is the same URL with `swerve=3375&swervefor=20`: the bars come back before the slick, the freeze `hold=hazard` still fires (the bike touched the slick), and the series climbs with worstStep 0 and no WIPEOUT. The crash needs the bars held through the contact, not the contact alone. `packages/sim/src/hazard-contact.test.ts` ("full lock across an oil slick at 180 km/h puts the rider down...") pins the exact thresholds.
- **Rock, slow: a stagger.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-7&before=8&kmh=15&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=4" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops by more than half in one step.
- **Rock, 70 km/h: a bump.** Run the same with `before=20&kmh=30&hold=near&holdafter=8`, then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops in one step by much less than the stagger above: the faster bike loses less, by the rules.
- **Rock, 150 km/h: a bump, no crash.** First establish contact. Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-7&before=40&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=hazard&holdafter=0" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 14` (the freeze is the contact; a timeout means the bike missed the rock and the step is inconclusive, not a pass), then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'` with the window raised to 60 samples (3 s) and `wipeout` tracked: `globalThis.__rushlineRelease(), new Promise((done) => { const speeds = []; let wipe = false; const id = setInterval(() => { wipe = wipe || document.body.innerText.includes("WIPEOUT"); speeds.push(Number(document.querySelector("[role=meter][aria-label=Speed]").getAttribute("aria-valuenow"))); if (speeds.length === 60) { clearInterval(id); done({ speeds: speeds.join(" "), worstStep: Math.min(...speeds.slice(1).map((speed, at) => speed - speeds[at])), wipeout: wipe }); } }, 50); })`. The proof run read 140 flat, a dip to 137, then climbing to 162 and 165 (worstStep -3, wipeout false). Then show the bike in the air: the same URL with `hold=air&holdafter=4`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 14`, `rv shot --run <run> rock150-air`. The frame shows the bike above the road with the rock under its rear wheel, its shadow detached and dust and sparks at the stone. The bike was on the rock, flew, and rode on: a contact freeze, a bike in the air, no WIPEOUT in 3 s, and the speed climbing again.
- **Rock, 215 km/h: a crash.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-7&before=40&kmh=215&straight&clear&prof&hold=crash&holdafter=20" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 14` and `rv shot --run <run> rock-crash`. It freezes about four seconds in with WIPEOUT over the road, the bike down and the rider in the air. The meter before the contact read 179 km/h for the 215 km/h request (see the speed gotcha below).
- **Pothole: a jolt.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-3&before=50&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=6" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops about 4% in one step (162 to 155 km/h, worstStep -7) and the rider rides on.
- **Missing one costs nothing.** `hold=nearMiss` alone does not prove it: `passesClose` in `packages/sim/src/hazards.ts` checks only the gap at that tick, so a bike that was staggered two ticks earlier can still fire it. Prove the pass from the speed series. Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-7&before=60&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&swerve=2510&swervefor=40&swerveto=0.5&hold=near&holdafter=25" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> miss-before`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The freeze is 25 m before the rock and the series covers the whole pass (about 0.6 s of the 2 s window). It shows no contact-sized step (-4 or lower); the swerve's drag alone costs at most 2 km/h. Then show the pass was close: the same URL with `hold=nearMiss&holdafter=2`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 12`, `rv shot --run <run> near-miss`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The freeze says the bike passed within 0.8 m; the frame shows the stone just beside and behind the bike, clear of both wheels; the series climbs with worstStep 0. Both runs must read no step: the first covers the pass, the second the moment the near-miss fired. With `swerveto=1` the bike clears it by more than 0.8 m and nothing freezes.
- **Frame rate with hazards on screen (informational, no verdict).** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-10&before=150&kmh=150&straight&prof" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> 'globalThis.__rushlineProfile.info().tick > 0'`, `rv sample --run <run> hazard-fps --seconds 4`. The window covers the whole approach to the slick, 150 m, with real traffic. Report the frames, fps, frame-time percentiles, JS per frame and draw calls it prints. ## Gotchas

- Hazard ids are per road and the flag looks them up in the race: `hazard=hazard-1` is a different hazard on every road, and a wrong id silently starts the player at `?at` (or the line). Take ids from the node command.
- Slag Row's `hazard-7`, a rock at 6,372 m x 1.41, is touched at 150 and 215 km/h from the flag's start. Use it for fast rock contact.
- `kmh=<n>` sets the starting speed to `n / 3.6` m/s with no cap (`dev-ride.ts` passes it straight to `setRiderSpeed`). The speed at contact is not the request: drag and the bike's own acceleration change it, and the meter read 140 km/h before the contact for `kmh=150` and 179 for `kmh=215` (the frozen frame shows that pre-contact reading; the series after release then climbs). Quote both the requested start and the speed you read near the contact.
- The page does not expose which effect a contact had (slip, stagger, bump, jolt), nor the lean or speed at the moment of contact. The recipe infers the effect from the freeze kind, the size of the speed step, and the picture, and the exact numbers live in `packages/sim/src/hazard-contact.test.ts`. The frozen frame's HUD shows the speed from before the contact; always release and sample.
- `?straight` holds the hazard's line only with throttle full. The bike's speed at contact differs from `kmh` in either direction (see the `kmh` gotcha above), so read the meter before the contact in the frozen frame instead of trusting the request.
- The pothole throw (above 175 km/h with the bars hard over) was not reached with `swerve` at 200 km/h: the pothole is 1 m wide and the lean did not build before the bike left it. `packages/sim/src/hazard-contact.test.ts` pins that case instead.
- `hold=` fires once per page load. Reopen the page for each step; `rv open` does that.
- A rock directly ahead of the bike is hidden behind it within about 10 m of the camera. Freeze at 22 m for a picture.
