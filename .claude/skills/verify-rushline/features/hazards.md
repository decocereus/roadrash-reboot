# Road hazards

Oil slicks, small rocks, and potholes lie on the road, mostly where a rider is setting up or leaving a bend or just past a crest. Nothing warns of them: no sign, no marker. A slick is a dark flat patch, a rock a grey stone, a pothole a dark hole. Riding straight over an oil slick is safe; a rock bumps the bike into the air and costs speed, and throws the rider only at about 210 km/h or when they are already steering hard; a pothole costs a few per cent of speed and throws only a bike leaned hard over above 175 km/h. A rider who misses a hazard loses nothing. Only riders meet hazards; traffic drives over them.

## Sub-features

- `hazard-placement` puts each hazard where the track schedules it, by road and level.
- `hazard-look` draws the slick, the rock, and the pothole on the road.
- `hazard-oil` is safe straight across and slips a bike that holds the bars hard over.
- `hazard-rock` staggers a slow bike and bumps a fast one into the air, and throws the rider at about 210 km/h.
- `hazard-pothole` jolts the bike and costs a few per cent of its speed.
- `hazard-miss` leaves a rider who passes beside a hazard with no consequence.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Ride any road at level 1 to 5. Level 1 has four to seven hazards per road; later levels add more, and every hazard from a lower level is in the same place with the same id.
- Breakwater Run's first hazard is a rock at 920 m. Its first oil slick is at 3,145 m, in the outer lane at the turn-in of a bend.
- Cut a bend close and the apex pothole is there; run wide on the exit and an oil slick or a rock is there.

## Driving it with rv

Preconditions:

- `rv doctor --run <run>` passes on a development build (every flag below is DEV only).
- Check the schedule first, from the checkout under test, with no browser: `node --input-type=module -e 'import { roads, trackFor } from "./packages/track/src/roads.ts"; for (const id of Object.keys(roads)) { const track = trackFor(id, 1); console.log(id, track.length, "bad:", track.hazards.filter((hazard) => hazard.s < 450 || hazard.s > track.length - 150 || Math.abs(hazard.x) > track.roadHalfWidth).length); for (const hazard of track.hazards) { console.log("  " + hazard.id + " " + hazard.kind + " " + hazard.setting + " s=" + Math.round(hazard.s) + " x=" + hazard.x.toFixed(2)); } }'`.

The hazard flags used below, all DEV: `hazard=<id>` puts the player on that hazard's line, `before=<m>` metres before it (default 150); `kmh=<n>` sets the starting speed; `straight` holds that line at full throttle; `clear` moves traffic out of the way; `hold=near&holdafter=<m>` freezes the race when the hazard is that many metres ahead; `hold=hazard` freezes it on the player's first contact with any hazard; `hold=nearMiss` freezes it when the player passes a hazard within 0.8 m without touching it; `hold=crash` freezes on a crash. `holdafter` on the event holds is a number of ticks (60 a second) after the event. All of them need `&prof` for `rv sample` and the tick. Each freeze sets `__rushlineHeld`; `rv wait --run <run> held` waits for it.

Read the speed with `document.querySelector("[role=meter][aria-label=Speed]").getAttribute("aria-valuenow")`. The page exposes no race state: it has no hazard list, no event log, and no lean. A frozen frame still shows the speed from before the contact, so read what a contact did by releasing and sampling the meter. Call this `SERIES` below: `globalThis.__rushlineRelease(), new Promise((done) => { const speeds = []; const id = setInterval(() => { speeds.push(Number(document.querySelector("[role=meter][aria-label=Speed]").getAttribute("aria-valuenow"))); if (speeds.length === 40) { clearInterval(id); done({ speeds: speeds.join(" "), worstStep: Math.min(...speeds.slice(1).map((speed, at) => speed - speeds[at])) }); } }, 50); })`. Pass it to `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. Speed climbs a few km/h a second under throttle, so a contact is a single step of -4 km/h or lower in `worstStep`; the proof runs without contact read 0, and steering drag alone cost at most 2 km/h in a step.

- **Where they lie.** Run the node command above. For Breakwater Run at level 1 it prints six hazards, which are `hazard-1` rock at 920 m x 0.84, `hazard-3` oil at 3,145 m x 5.73, `hazard-5` rock at 4,680 m x 1.03, `hazard-7` rock at 6,946 m x 0.32, `hazard-8` oil at 7,488 m x 1.49, and `hazard-9` oil at 8,081 m x 5.41, with `bad: 0`. Compare every road with the level-1 table under "Where hazards lie" in `docs/design/tracks.md`; each hazard's kind, setting, and position must match and `bad` must be 0. Change `1` to another level to see more hazards, with the level-1 ones unmoved.
- **The game has the same hazard.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-3&before=60&kmh=100&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> placement-oil`. It freezes about four seconds after the page opens. An id the race does not have never freezes, because the player is then left at the start line: a freeze proves `race.hazards` holds `hazard-3` and that it lies 22 m ahead of a bike placed 60 m before 3,145 m and riding in its lane.
- **It draws: oil.** The frame from the step above shows a small dark flat slick on the road about 22 m ahead in the right-hand lane, on the bike's line, with the bike near the right edge line.
- **It draws: rock.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-7&before=60&kmh=60&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> draws-rock`. A small grey lump sits on the centre line ahead of the bike. It is a few pixels wide at 22 m by design (hazards are not made to stand out), so look for it in the full-size PNG; the near-miss frame below shows the rock at full size.
- **It draws: pothole.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-3&before=60&kmh=60&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=22" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv shot --run <run> draws-pothole`. A dark thin hole lies on the road ahead, close to the centre line, at night; it is the hardest to see of the three.
- **Oil, straight over it.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-3&before=60&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=hazard&holdafter=10" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20` (the freeze proves the bike touched a hazard), `rv shot --run <run> oil-contact`, then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The slick lies behind the bike in the frame, the speed series keeps climbing with no step down (168 up to 182 km/h, worstStep 0, in the proof run), and no WIPEOUT appears.
- **Rock, slow: a stagger.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-1&before=8&kmh=15&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=4" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops by more than half in one step: 42 to 18 km/h in the proof run (worstStep -24).
- **Rock, 70 km/h: a bump.** Run the same with `before=20&kmh=30&hold=near&holdafter=8`, then `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops about a tenth in one step (68 to 63 km/h, worstStep -5), much less than the stagger above: the faster bike loses less, by the rules.
- **Rock, 150 km/h: no crash.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-7&before=40&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=crash&holdafter=20" --until race` and `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 14`. It times out: the rider rode on.
- **Rock, 215 km/h: a crash.** Run the same with `kmh=215`, then `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 14` and `rv shot --run <run> rock-crash`. It freezes about four seconds in, with WIPEOUT over the road, the bike down and the rider in the air.
- **Pothole: a jolt.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=slag-row&hazard=hazard-3&before=50&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&hold=near&holdafter=6" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 20`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The speed drops about 4% in one step (162 to 155 km/h, worstStep -7) and the rider rides on.
- **Missing one costs nothing.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-1&before=60&kmh=150&straight&clear&prof&swerve=870&swervefor=40&swerveto=0.5&hold=nearMiss&holdafter=2" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> held --timeout 12`, `rv shot --run <run> near-miss`, `rv js --run <run> '<SERIES>'`. The freeze proves the bike passed within 0.8 m of the rock without touching it. The frame shows the stone just beside and behind the bike, clear of both wheels; the series climbs (156 up to 186 km/h) with worstStep 0. With `swerveto=1` the bike clears it by more than 0.8 m and nothing freezes.
- **Frame rate with hazards on screen.** Run `rv open --run <run> "/?ride=breakwater-run&hazard=hazard-3&before=150&kmh=150&straight&prof" --until race`, `rv wait --run <run> 'globalThis.__rushlineProfile.info().tick > 0'`, `rv sample --run <run> hazard-fps --seconds 4`. The window covers the whole approach to the slick, 150 m, with real traffic. The proof run read 240 frames, 59.9 fps, p95 17.3 ms, worst 24.2 ms, 2.08 ms of JS per frame. Other agents load this machine, so judge the budget from the median and p95, not from one worst frame.

## Gotchas

- Hazard ids are per road and the flag looks them up in the race: `hazard=hazard-1` is a different hazard on every road, and a wrong id silently starts the player at `?at` (or the line). Take ids from the node command.
- `hazard-1` on Breakwater Run is never touched at 180 km/h or faster from the flag's start (no freeze in four runs at 180, 190, 210, and 240), though `hazard-5` and `hazard-7` are touched at 200. Use `hazard-7` for fast rock contact, and do not read a missing freeze at speed on `hazard-1` as proof the rock does nothing. The reason was not investigated.
- `kmh` above about 205 is capped by the bike; the HUD never read more than 215.
- The page does not expose which effect a contact had (slip, stagger, bump, jolt), nor the lean or speed at the moment of contact. The recipe infers the effect from the freeze kind, the size of the speed step, and the picture, and the exact numbers live in `packages/sim/src/hazard-contact.test.ts`. The frozen frame's HUD shows the speed from before the contact; always release and sample.
- `?straight` holds the hazard's line only with throttle full; the bike then rides about 5 to 15 km/h faster than `kmh` by the time it arrives, so a "150 km/h" run reads 154 to 168 km/h at the contact.
- The pothole throw (above 175 km/h with the bars hard over) was not reached with `swerve` at 200 km/h: the pothole is 1 m wide and the lean did not build before the bike left it. `packages/sim/src/hazard-contact.test.ts` pins that case instead.
- `hold=` fires once per page load. Reopen the page for each step; `rv open` does that.
- A rock directly ahead of the bike is hidden behind it within about 10 m of the camera. Freeze at 22 m for a picture.
