# Junctions and crossings

A road declares junctions and pedestrian crossings in its plan's `crossings`. A junction has a cross road with arms on one or both sides, a zebra each side of its box and, when it has lights, a stop line and a mast-arm signal for each direction of the main road and a short signal on each arm. A pedestrian crossing has one zebra and, with lights, the same two mast-arm signals. The lights cycle in 23 s at a junction (main green 9 s, amber 2.5 s, all red 1 s, cross green 7 s, amber 2.5 s, all red 1 s) from the race tick and the crossing's offset. Main-road traffic stops at the stop line on a red, and on an amber it can stop for, and queues behind it. A car too close to stop runs the amber. Riders and the pilot ride through.

## Sub-features

- `crossing-format` compiles each road's crossings and refuses one on a bend, a crest, near the start or finish, or within 120 m of another.
- `crossing-clearance` keeps hazards off a crossing's reserved stretch and props and city blocks off a cross road's arms.
- `crossing-look` draws the arms, zebras, stop lines and signal heads.
- `crossing-lights` lights the green, amber or red lamp of each head from `signalPhaseAt`.
- `traffic-at-lights` stops main-road traffic at a red and queues it behind the stop line (`stopAtLights` in `packages/sim/src/traffic.ts`).
- `cross-cars` drives cars down the cross roads, across the box or round into a main-road lane on a T, and hits riders by speed and lean (`packages/sim/src/cross-traffic.ts`, `hitCrossCar` in `packages/sim/src/rider-collisions.ts`).

- `pedestrians` walks people across zebras, unmarked crossings and jaywalking spots in city regions, drawn as six skinned bodies in eight outfits, with poses baked into one texture, in `apps/web/src/game/walkers.ts`. They cross on the main road's red or into a gap in the cars, cars stop for them, and a rider who meets one takes a soft rock.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Breakwater Run has `harbour-cross` at 1,700 m (two lanes each way, both arms, lights), `market-cross` at 6,000 m (one lane each way, a right arm only, lights), and `pier-crossing` at 7,850 m (a signalled zebra). List them from the checkout under test with no browser: `node --input-type=module -e 'import { trackFor } from "./packages/track/src/roads.ts"; for (const level of [1, 4]) for (const c of trackFor("breakwater-run", level).crossings) console.log(level, c.id, c.s, c.fromS, c.toS, c.road?.arms ?? "no road", c.lights ? "lights" : "no lights", c.walk);'`.

## Gotchas

- `?at` is metres along the road, and the camera sits behind the bike, so `at` 25 to 30 m short of a crossing's centre frames it best.
- The lamps are small at 30 m. Crop the head from the full-size PNG (`sips -c 260 260 --cropOffset 240 1470 <png> --out <crop>` at rv's default page size) to read the colour.
- The proofs in `packages/track/src/crossings.test.ts`, `packages/sim/src/signals.test.ts` and `packages/sim/src/junction-traffic.test.ts` pin the placement rules, the cycle and the queue. `packages/sim/src/cross-traffic.test.ts` pins the cross cars, their contact grading and the pilot's reaction. `packages/sim/src/pedestrians.test.ts` and `packages/track/src/walk-sites.test.ts` pin the walkers and where they stand.
- A `hazard` id the race does not have leaves the bike at the start.
- `pnpm perf` runs `road-life-v1` from `tools/perf/src/node-road-life.ts`: Breakwater Run on seed 8, ticks 2,400 to 3,000, with `harbour-cross` full of racers and at least three people on the road. `benchSetup` empties `walkSites` on the Slag Row scenarios, so those still compare against side A, and only `road-life-v1` carries the cost of walkers.
