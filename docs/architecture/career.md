# Career

`packages/career` owns every career rule: money, tiers, qualification, the shop, the field draw, rival memory, season points, story flags, settlement, and the save format. Like the simulation it is plain data and plain functions, with no DOM, clock, or unseeded randomness. React renders what it returns and never changes a rule; if the career should behave differently, change `packages/career`. It reads the cast, the bikes, and `RaceSetup` from `packages/sim` and the road ids and levels from `packages/track`. It never looks up or compiles a track: the caller passes one in.

`CareerState` is the save. It holds cash, level, the bike ridden and every bike owned, the carried weapon, the roads qualified at each tier with the best place, the record, season points, what each rival remembers, story flags, the three newest board notes, the active-race record, the last settled result, and the game-over flag. Bike condition is not in it, because bikes are repaired between races. Every function returns a new state and leaves the old one alone, so a screen can ask what a bill would do before the player agrees to it.

A career race runs like this. `setupFor(rules, career, roadId, seed, track)` draws `fieldSize - 1` rivals, puts each on their bike and weapon for the tier, sets grudges and aggression bonuses from memory, and adds the tier's conditions, police and pacing. `startRace` writes the active-race record and clears the last result. The simulation clears its events every tick, so the runner calls `recordTick(log, state, setup)` after each `stepRace`. The log counts knockdowns, stolen weapons, hits, and busts, with the tick and the kilometre of each, and freezes the result at the tick the player's `finish`, `busted`, or `wrecked` event fires. Nothing after that tick changes it. The arrest's position and the confiscated weapon come from the `busted` event. `outcomeOf` returns the frozen result, or a quit if the player has not settled yet. `settleRace(rules, career, outcome)` applies it once: prize with the repeat-run rule, repair or fine, the envelope, the debt check with repossession and game over, qualification, tier-up, the ending, season points, memory, and the carried weapon. It clears the active-race record in the same step and returns a `Ledger` for Results.

**Race rules.** Whether the celebrities ride, the field size and the bike matches come from the server. A bike match moves the rivals on one bike up to the player's when the player rides that faster bike at that tier, and it applies only to a career field, because rooms leave it empty and put every racer on the room's bike. `launchRules` in `packages/career/src/rules.ts` holds the values, ten racers, and `apps/web/src/server/race-rules.ts` serves them from `GET /api/race-rules` uncached. The browser reads them once at boot through `apps/web/src/rules/load.ts`, which decodes them with `RaceRulesSchema`, and every career call that draws a field or opens a road takes them as its first argument. They are fixed for the page load, so a career and the race in flight never see them change. The simulation takes no rules and keeps only the ceiling, `MOST_RACERS`. Replay fixtures, perf scenarios, `?stress`, and the career tests pass `recordedRules` from `bench.ts`, fifteen racers and no celebrities.

**Tiers.** `difficulties` in `packages/sim/src/difficulty.ts` holds what each of the three tiers does to a race: how many of a road's hazards are placed, traffic density, slow convoys, hit damage, police, pacing and rival skill. `setupFor` copies a tier's values into the `RaceSetup`, and `createRace` refuses values outside `ruleRanges`, so a race's values are locked and checked at its start and can later arrive from a server. The compiled `Track` is the same at every tier: it holds every hazard candidate with a `rarity`, its rank among the road's candidates as a share, and a race places those under its `hazardShare`. Shop stock, story chapters, Vic's cards and Dee's alliance arrive by tier in `packages/career`. Rooms take a tier from `publicRace` in `launchRoomConfig` (`apps/rooms/src/room-config.ts`). `docs/design/game-design.md` section 4 has the table.

**Race identity.** Every started race has a `raceId`, the career's count of starts. `startRace` puts it in the active-race record, `setupFor` puts it in the setup, and the race log stamps it into the outcome. `settleRace` accepts an outcome only when its `raceId`, road, tier, and seed match the active race, so a result from an earlier race cannot pay again, even on the same road with the same seed. A refusal is a value, not a throw: the `Settlement` has `settled: false`, a null ledger, the career unchanged, and a `reason` of `noActiveRace`, `gameOver`, or `wrongRace`.

**Quitting.** `quitRace(rules, career, outcome)` takes the live outcome from `outcomeOf` at the tick of the quit. Every rival is classified where they are at that tick, the player is placed behind everyone still racing and counts as wrecked, and the full repair bill is charged with no ad offer. Season points, memory, and board notes from the part of the race that was ridden all count. A player who quits after their race has already settled gets that settled result.

**Reloading.** `resumeCareer` is what the web app calls on a loaded save. If the save holds an active-race record, the race itself is gone, so it settles an empty outcome as a wreck at the full bill, once: no places, no points, and nothing remembered. It adds Vic's note to the board. With no active-race record it returns a `noActiveRace` refusal and the save unchanged.

**The last result.** Settlement stores `lastResult` in the career: the `Ledger` and a summary with the race's `raceId` and settlement tick, a row for each racer (name, bike, place, fate, finish tick or metres left, and prize), the incidents with each rival, the arrest (officer, their line, kilometre, confiscated weapon, fine), and the wreck (bike and kilometre). It is in the save, so a refresh during the Finish animation or on Results still has every number those screens show. The next `startRace` clears it.

**The save.** `parseCareer` checks every field and range and returns an error value for a bad save; it does not throw, whatever the text holds. The error has a kind and the path of the first field that failed. `invalid` is a value of the wrong kind or out of range, or a road or racer the game does not have. `inconsistent` is a save that contradicts itself: an active race on a bike other than the one ridden, at another tier, with the wrong `raceId`, in a career that is over, or beside a stored result; a stored result that is not the last race started; a season or memory stamp later than the number of starts; or a bike ridden that is not in the garage. Reading and writing `localStorage` is the web app's job.

**The board.** Notes from a race are ordered by the tick they happened at, newest first, ahead of older notes, and the board keeps three. A repossession or a new bike at the garage happens at settlement and goes on top.

The design left some things open. These are the choices made here:

- A knockdown is a `crash` whose `by` names the other rider, whatever the cause except `rider`, so a rival punched into a car counts and a rival who runs into the back of the player does not. The same holds the other way: a rival who punches the player into traffic is put down for the knockdown. The log keeps the kilometre of the last knockdown each way, and a rival who knocked the player down gets a `knockedPlayerDown` board note.
- A rival placed ahead of the player won by a wheel when the player finished within 30 ticks after them. A rival placed behind lost by a wheel when they finished within 30 ticks after the player, or were still riding within 25 m at settlement.
- A rival's aggression bonus applies toward the player at any size and either sign. The grudge flag only decides whether the rival prefers the player as a target.
- The save version is 6, and `parseCareer` refuses a save of any other version as `wrongVersion` rather than migrating it. A save written before a rival joined the game loads with blank memory and season rows for that rival.
- Police ride the Sesta at tiers 1 and 2 and the Nera at tier 3, so the bike is always faster than the tier's pursuit speed.
- Selling is `sellBike`: the player gets the trade-in value and rides the Gutter Pup. It is the only shop action open to a player in debt.
- The envelope can be taken until the player's first start on Breakwater Run at tier 2 or the move to tier 3, whichever comes first.
- Once an ending is earned, no further career race starts.
